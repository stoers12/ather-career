**Ather Privacy Policy**
**Version:** {{version}} · **Effective:** {{effective_date}}

### 1. Who we are
Ather is a platform that helps you build your professional profile and portfolio and, if you choose, publish it. The Ather platform, operated by Momen Al-Omari, is responsible for your data.
Privacy contact: {{contact}}.

### 2. In short
- We collect only what you write and what we need to run the platform safely.
- **We do not sell your data, and we use no third-party advertising, tracking or analytics tools.**
- **We never store your password.** Sign-in is through Google via Auth0.
- You decide what visitors see. Everything in the Evidence Hub is private to you.
- You can download your data or delete your account at any time.

### 3. What we collect
**a) Your account:**
- Your Auth0 account identifier, which contains no password.
- One fact from Google: whether your email is verified.

**b) What you write:**
- **Profile:** name, title, headline, location, about, work description and photo.
- **Contact methods you choose:** email, phone, and LinkedIn, GitHub and website links.
- **Projects:** title, category, description, technologies, links and images.
- **Experience and skills.**
- **Evidence Hub:** evidence text, sources, and the analysis and recommendations derived from them. **This is fully private.**
- **Preferences:** language, appearance and text size.

**c) Messages visitors send you:**
- When a visitor contacts you through your page, we store their name, email and message so they reach your inbox.

**d) Technical security data:**
- **To prevent abuse** (such as repeated sign-in attempts), we compute an **irreversible fingerprint** of your IP address. **We do not store the IP address itself.**
- **Server logs** contain only the time and outcome of a request, **with no IP address and no page path.**
- **Security event logs** (such as "denied sign-in") contain only your internal account number, if any, and the event type.

**e) Cookies:**
- **Strictly necessary cookies only:** the sign-in session and protection against forged requests (CSRF).
- No advertising or analytics cookies.

### 4. Why we use it
- To run your account and show your public page the way you choose.
- To analyse your evidence inside the Evidence Hub, **for you only.**
- To deliver visitor messages to you.
- To protect the platform and its users from abuse and attacks.
- To answer you when you contact us.

**We do not use your data to train AI models, and we do not share it with advertisers.**

### 5. What visitors see
- **Visitors see only what you publish:** your profile, published projects, experience, skills, and the contact methods you made visible.
- **Visitors never see:**
  - drafts and unpublished projects;
  - the Evidence Hub and its analysis;
  - your messages;
  - any contact method you hid.
- If you unpublish your page, it disappears from visitors immediately.

### 6. Who we share data with
Only service providers that run the platform for us, and only as much as they need:

| Provider | Role |
|---|---|
| Auth0 (Okta) | Sign-in |
| Google | Sign-in with a Google account |
| Oracle Cloud | Server and database hosting |
| Cloudflare | Protection and speed |

Some of them may process data outside your country. We choose providers that follow recognised protection standards.
We may disclose data where the law requires it.

### 7. How long we keep it
- **While your account exists.**
- **When you request account deletion**:
  - your page disappears from visitors immediately;
  - you have **30 days** to change your mind;
  - then we permanently delete your data and images, **and your Auth0 account too.**
- **Encrypted backups** may hold your data for up to **30 further days**, then they are erased automatically.
- **Security logs** are kept for a limited time: 90 days.

### 8. Security
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- No stored passwords.
- Strict isolation between accounts.
- Encrypted backups.
- Limits on sign-in attempts.

No system is 100% secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

### 9. Your rights
You can:
- **Access and download** a copy of your data.
- **Correct** your data at any time from your account.
- **Delete** your account and data.
- **Control visibility** by hiding any item or unpublishing your page.
- **Object or complain** by writing to {{contact}}. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

We answer requests within 30 days.

### 10. Age
During the beta, Ather is **for people aged 18 or older.**

### 11. Changes
- For significant changes, we raise the version number and tell you inside the platform before they take effect.
- We record the version you accepted.

### 12. Governing law
This policy is governed by Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023. We also respect the rights of users in the European Union under the GDPR where it applies.
